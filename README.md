# 📊 FY2026 Commercial Sales & Revenue Performance Dashboard

An end-to-end interactive sales analytics dashboard built in Microsoft Excel to evaluate commercial revenue trends, regional performance, and multi-channel profitability for FY2026[cite: 6].

---

## 🚀 Key Business Metrics (KPIs)
* **Total Revenue:** $2.45M[cite: 6]
* **Total Orders:** 2,000 orders fulfilled[cite: 6]
* **Quantity Sold:** 5,552 units[cite: 6]
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** $1.23K[cite: 6]

---

## 📈 Key Insights & Analysis
* **Sales Channel Breakdown:** Online channels (32%) and Corporate Sales (29%) drove the majority of total revenue, followed by Retail Stores (26%) and Marketplaces (13%)[cite: 6].
* **Regional Performance:** North region led in revenue generation ($562K), followed by Central ($557K) and East ($468K)[cite: 6].
* **Product Category Trends:** Computers generated the highest revenue ($1.3M+), with Gaming Laptops ($433K) and Laptop Air 13 ($274K) being the top revenue-generating individual products[cite: 6].
* **Monthly Trajectory:** Peak revenue periods were observed in November ($274K) and June ($259K)[cite: 6].

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Excel Techniques Used
* **Data Modeling & Summarization:** Structured PivotTables for multi-dimensional aggregation across regions, categories, and channels[cite: 6].
* **Interactive Data Filtering:** Synchronized Slicers (Region, Product Category, Sales Channel, Customer Type)[cite: 6].
* **Dynamic Time Slicers:** Date Timelines for granular filtering by Year, Quarter, Month, and Day[cite: 6].
* **Data Visualizations:** Line charts for monthly seasonality, bar/column charts for regional and product distribution, and donut charts for sales channel contributions[cite: 6].
